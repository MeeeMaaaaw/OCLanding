Hotspot, vendo and PPPoE on one MikroTik.

One-Controller runs as a container on RouterOS. Vouchers, coin-slot vendos and PPPoE billing are managed from one admin panel, with no separate server.

See pricingWatch install tutorial

What it does

For hotspot, vendo and PPPoE operators.

1

Runs on your router

Installs as a RouterOS container. No extra server or hardware.

2

Hotspot vouchers

Generate, print and track vouchers. Customers log in through a captive portal.

3

Coin-slot vendo

ESP-based coin slot. Coins are credited to the customer’s session automatically.

4

PPPoE Manager

Accounts, profiles, servers and billing. Overdue subscribers see a disconnection notice and can send a payment reference, which goes straight to the Payments page in PPPoE Manager.

5

Online activation

Each license is bound to one router’s serial number. Activation needs an internet connection.

6

Manual updates

Updates are manual. Nothing changes until you install it.
